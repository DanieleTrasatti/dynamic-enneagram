Welcome to a journey of self-knowledge through one of the most fascinating systems for understanding human personality: the Enneagram.

What is it?

The Enneagram is an ancient diagram — a circle crossed by a triangle and a six-pointed figure — that became, in the twentieth century, a precise psychological map. It describes the nine strategies the human being learns to survive emotionally in childhood, and the way back to what came before: your Essence, the authentic and undivided nature each person carries.

According to this model, Essence is the pure, undivided nature of the individual, which becomes clouded and fragmented in childhood; around this loss the personality is built as a survival strategy, crystallised in a dominant Enneatype with its passion and fixation. The version presented here follows the classic Enneagram of Personality method, in the tradition of Oscar Ichazo and Claudio Naranjo and popularised by Don Richard Riso and Russ Hudson.

Three good reasons to explore it

  • It helps you recognise the automatic pattern that drives your reactions, often without you noticing.
  • It shows you that under every defence there is an understandable wound, and under every wound a gift.
  • It points you to a concrete direction of growth: every type has its own path of liberation.

Where to begin?

The site journey starts with the "what": the symbol — circle, triangle, hexad and the nine points — with its deeper theory (the Law of Three and the Law of Seven) gathered in an in-depth chapter you can also skip. It continues with the "dynamics": how the model of Essence, wound and personality works; then the good news arrives right away: the "how you grow", the path of integration, because your type is not a life sentence. Then the mirror: the nine types — recognising yourself in a strategy is worth a thousand definitions — and the "how it moves": wings, stress and integration points, triads. The circle will close with the Origins and, when you are ready to look in the mirror, the Test: the step where the journey returns to you. But this is not a one-way path: you can jump straight to the station that calls you and move freely between pages, as on the Enneagram circle.
