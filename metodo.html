How it works: from Essence to personality

Essence: what you were before you "adapted"

Every child is born with a whole and present nature: Essence. It is the root of spontaneity, joy, the capacity to love and to perceive reality without filters. It is not an abstract mystical concept: it is the direct experience of being alive and whole, before fear teaches us to defend ourselves.

The primal wound

In childhood, when the environment does not fully welcome some aspects of the child, a fracture occurs: the little one senses that to be loved and protected they must give up a part of themselves. Around this loss a primal wound forms — a crucial need that seems to be missing (love, safety, worth, presence…) — and with it a strategy to obtain or compensate it.

The personality as a survival strategy

Over time the strategy crystallises into a stable structure: the personality. Each personality is organised around three interconnected elements:

  • The passion (or vice): a recurring dominant emotion — anger, fear, sadness, pride… — that fuels the type and makes it act "on autopilot".
  • The fixation: the habitual mental script, the repetitive way the type interprets reality and justifies its reactions.
  • The defence mechanism: the psychological operation that protects the wound (identification, projection, repression, idealisation…).

Nine strategies, one for each point of the symbol

Each child develops a dominant strategy — their Enneatype — which becomes the centre of gravity of the personality. It is neither a choice nor a destiny: it is an intelligent adaptation to early conditions. And precisely because it is an adaptation, it can be observed, understood and lightened.

In short: according to this model, Essence is the pure, undivided nature of the individual, which becomes clouded and fragmented in childhood; around this loss the personality is built as a survival strategy, crystallised in a dominant Enneatype with its passion and fixation.

"Your type does not describe what you are: it describes what you do to protect yourself."
