How to answer the questionnaire

Answer based on how you would behave at this moment of your life in the situations proposed by the questionnaire.
Do NOT answer thinking about how you think you are, how you used to be or how you think you should be. Answer based on how you are in this period of your life. Do not overthink it.

Within the questionnaire you will be shown various statements: each describes typical traits and attitudes of the nine Enneatypes. For each of them you will give

The statements you will meet fall essentially into three categories: those that do not resemble you at all — answer NO to these; those that describe you moderately — answer MAYBE; those that describe you well — answer YES.
